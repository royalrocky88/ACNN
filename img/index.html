Aims & Objectives

The challenges posed by nanoscience and nanotechnology cannot be answered solely by techniques and methods derived from a single science or technology discipline. Instead, it requires a combination of diverse, but inter-related techniques spanning many disciplines that forms the core of an emerging discipline of Nanoscience and Nanotechnology.

To provide

high quality postgraduate teaching and research leading to the degree :
* M.Sc. (Nano Science & Nanotechnology)
* M.Tech. (Nano Science & Nanotechnology)
* Ph.D. (Nano Science & Nanotechnology)

Free hand to carry out state-of-art goal oriented Research & Development (Multidisciplinary). Attention shall be paid towards the development of Prototype and Commercialization.

Provide Entrepreneurship Development Programme as well as Short-Term Training Programme in Nanotechnology to produce skilled human resource and generate self-employment in the state. Effort shall also be made to promote nanotechnology based medium/small/cottage industries.
